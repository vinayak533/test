# Misty Mountain Plantation Resort

> *An old story waiting for new eyes*

Misty Mountain Plantation Resort is set amidst a lush 600-acre estate in the idyllic village of Peermade on the Sahyadri Hills of the Western Ghats of Kerala. A peaceful escape nestled in the greenery of Kuttikkanam, offering estate life experiences from tea factory tours to guided crop walks with a resident naturalist.

## 4. Activities

The site advertises **8+ activities**; 9 are individually described:

1. **The Orthodox Tea Tour** — A guided jeep tour of the estate covering every stage of the tea-making process, starting with the leaf itself, plus the estate's other crops and wildlife. Includes a factory tour, tea tasting, and lunch at the lounge; begins after breakfast in your cabin.
2. **Jeep Safari** — Explore the rugged terrain of the biodiverse Western Ghats, observing native flora and fauna and learning how the estate's crops are farmed. Includes a visit to the natural waterfall on the property, most spectacular after the monsoon.
3. **Plantation Walks** — A leisurely guided stroll through the plantations with crisp mountain air and birdsong, learning the nuances of farming from resident guides.
4. **Cycling** — Bicycles are available on the property for a relaxed ride along the estate trails.
5. **Fishing** — Spend time by the tranquil water reserve at Misty Mountain; guides help set guests up with fishing rods and bait.
6. **Kayaking** — Paddle across the calm waters of the nearby dam reservoir in stable kayaks suited to beginners and experts alike, spotting birds and fish along the way.
7. **Barbecue Night** — An evening barbecue under the stars with chef-grilled specialties, live music, laughter, stargazing and storytelling. Suited to couples, families or groups.
8. **Campfire** — A crackling campfire in a safe, scenic clearing with plush seating, blankets and acoustic performances - roasting marshmallows, sharing tales and hot beverages under Peermade's clear skies.
9. **Golf Room** — A Golf Room featuring a custom virtual golf simulator - a relaxing indoor alternative to the outdoor adventures, suitable for all ages.

**Also mentioned:** Tea factory visit, Birdwatching, Stargazing and storytelling, Live / acoustic music

- Activities are subject to weather conditions. Prebooking activities carries a 10% discount.
- No activity prices are published on the website.
